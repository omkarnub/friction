TWO WAYS TO MEASURE μ

Both setups isolate the limiting static coefficient of friction between a block and a plane. Measured accurately on the same material pair, both converge to the same μ.

METHOD 01 GRAVITY-DRIVEN · NO EXTERNAL PULL

Angle of Repose

Slowly elevate one end of the hinged inclined plane carrying block W with zero external pull until the block just breaks free. That critical inclination is the angle of repose (α).

  1. 01
    Seat the block cleanly on the horizontal test plane at 0°.
  2. 02
    Increase tilt angle α smoothly without jarring the base.
  3. 03
    Read α the exact instant static friction yields to gravity.
GOVERNING EQUATION
μ = tan α
Launch Simulator
METHOD 02 PULLEY-LOADED · FIXED INCLINE θ

Friction Plane Method

Lock the plane at a fixed angle θ (including 0° horizontal). Route a cord from block W over a frictionless pulley to a hanging pan and add slotted weights until pull force P initiates upward motion.

  1. 01
    Lock the plane at a chosen inclination θ and weigh block W.
  2. 02
    Add calibrated weights to pan P in fine increments.
  3. 03
    Record limiting pull P the instant the block begins ascending.
GOVERNING EQUATION
μ = (P − W sin θ) / (W cos θ)

02

Operating Method A: Angle of Repose

Measuring limiting static friction by elevating the plane until sliding initiates

Determine the critical repose angle α where gravity overcomes limiting static friction with zero external pull:

  • Select Material Pair: Choose from 9 standard engineering pairs (e.g., Wood on Wood, Wood on Steel, Glass on Glass, Rubber on Concrete).
  • Adjust Block Weight (W): Vary normal load from 1.0 N to 10.0 N. Notice that while normal force N = W cos α scales with weight, the slip angle stays invariant.
  • Tilt the Incline (α): Drag the angle slider or use the − / + steppers (0.5° increments) to elevate the plane smoothly.
  • Inspect Force Vectors: Watch normal reaction N, down-slope gravity W sin α, and resisting friction f_s update in real time.
  • Detect Slip & Record: At tan α = μ_s, the block breaks equilibrium and slides. Click Add to Data Log to save the trial.
03

Operating Method B: Friction Plane with Pulley & Pan

Measuring friction using calibrated slotted weights on a hanging pan

Switch to the Friction Plane tab to run the classic pulley-and-pan mechanical bench:

  • Set Plane Angle (θ): Use 0° for a horizontal bench or lock any incline up to 60°.
  • Set Block Weight (W): Configure the test block mass resting on the plane.
  • Load Pan Effort (P): Click the slotted-weight chips (+5g, +10g, +20g, +50g) or drag the slider to load the hanging pan.
  • Limiting Equilibrium: When P ≥ W sin θ + μ_s W cos θ, the block begins moving up the plane and evaluates:
    μ = (P − W sin θ) / (W cos θ)   (or μ = P / W when θ = 0°)
  • Log Reading: Click Add to Data Log to transfer the trial into your observation table.
04

Observation Data Log, Verification & PDF Report

Reviewing statistical error against handbook ranges and exporting lab reports

Open Data Log to audit recorded trials and export formatted deliverables:

  • Interactive Trial Ledger: Lists each observation with Trial #, Angle, Weight W, Pull P, calculated μ, and validation status.
  • Method & Material Filter: Isolate specific surface pairs or switch between Angle of Repose and Friction Plane records.
  • Automated Telemetry: Computes Mean μ, Reference Range, Percentage Error (|μ_mean − μ_ref| / μ_ref × 100%), and Range Check status.
  • Export PDF / CSV: Download a formatted lab report PDF or raw CSV spreadsheet for further plotting.
